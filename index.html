Eating in Paris

It's easier than people think, though some of it only makes sense once you've been caught out a few times.

Shopping

Monoprix, Franprix, and Carrefour all sell tofu and plant milk. Organic shops like Naturalia and Biocoop have more choice but cost more. On labels, look for sans produits laitiers or végétal.

Restaurants

Falafel, Indian, Ethiopian, and Vietnamese places are usually the safest and cheapest. Say végétalien, not végétarien, or you may get dairy and eggs anyway.

Bakeries

Vegan croissants do exist, but they're mostly at the dedicated vegan bakeries. Ask us where.

Why

The environment

Producing animal products takes a lot more land, water, and energy than producing plants, and the emissions are higher too.

Animals

Not eating animals means fewer of them are raised and killed for food. For many of us this is the main reason.

Honestly, the food

A lot of us ended up cooking more and trying things we'd never have bothered with before.
