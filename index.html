Eating vegan in Paris

Paris has become a great city for plant-based food. A few tips to get you started.

🛒 Groceries

Most supermarkets (Monoprix, Franprix, Carrefour) carry plant milks, tofu and vegan spreads. Organic shops like Naturalia and Biocoop go further. Check labels for "sans produits laitiers" or the "végétal" mark.

🍽️ Eating out

Falafel, Indian, Ethiopian and Vietnamese places are reliable cheap options. Ask "Est-ce que c'est végétalien ?" since végétarien may still include dairy or eggs.

🥐 Pastries

Yes, vegan croissants exist. Dedicated vegan boulangeries and cafés across the city make them. Ask the club for our favourites.

Why vegan?

For the planet

Plant-based diets generally use far less land and water and produce fewer greenhouse gas emissions than diets rich in animal products.

For animals

Choosing plants is the most direct way to reduce the number of animals involved in the food system.

For you

Well-planned vegan diets can be nutritionally complete, and cooking with whole plant foods is a great way to try new flavours.
