Vegan Dinner Club

The aim of this club is to provide plant-based meals for its members.

Any student attending The American University of Paris is able to become a member of this club by requesting membership by contacting any of the executive members of VEGAN DINNER CLUB. Members must attend at least one event per academic year in order to preserve membership. Participants cannot bring food with ingredients that originate from animals to any event organized by VEGAN DINNER CLUB. Any individual expressing prejudice towards others or who has disruptive behavior that affects the club and/or the members of the club is not welcome to join or to remain in the club.

A member of the executive board can formally resign by submitting a written resignation to the club president.
